Our Men's Ministry gathers for Bible study, prayer meetings and fellowship, helping men apply biblical principles to work, family and personal growth.

The men of DCC also serve our wider community — including helping to run the free Saturday breakfast for people experiencing homelessness, where a warm meal opens the door to sharing God's Word.
