Children's church combines age-appropriate Bible teaching, informal activities and opportunities for children to use their gifts to serve God.

All children's activities are led by trusted volunteers and run in line with DCC's safeguarding policy. Speak to a member of our Welcome Team on arrival and they will help you get your children settled.
