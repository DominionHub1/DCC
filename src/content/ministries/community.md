Our Community Ministry mobilises the church to meet physical, emotional and spiritual needs across Peterborough — through our Saturday Breakfast Morning, food drives, clothing donations, educational programmes and shelter support, working alongside local organisations.

If you'd like to serve, we would love to have you on the team.
